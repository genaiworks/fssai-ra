How much oversight can you actually supply?

Every governed agent architecture routes consequential actions to a named human. Almost none of them say what happens when there are more actions than the humans can read. This works out your ceiling from numbers you already have.

Nothing you type leaves this page. There is no network call, no storage, and no analytics — which matters, because working out that you are over capacity should not require telling anyone.

What each answer commits you to

If your gap isThen one of these is true, and you should say which
at or below zero You can review what you send. Publish the ceiling anyway, because the number moves the first time someone automates a new workflow into the same queue.
above zero One of four things: you hire, you narrow what counts as consequential, you accept a queue and tell people how long it is — or the approvals above the line stop being review and become signatures. The fourth happens by default if you do not choose one of the first three.

There is no fifth option where the reviewers simply go faster. If the reading time you entered is real, going faster means not reading; and an approval issued without reading is exactly the failure this architecture cannot detect from the inside — every digest still binds, every signature still verifies, and the record still says a human approved it.

Turning this into an enforced control

The ceiling above is arithmetic. It becomes a control when the system refuses to issue an approval beyond it, rather than recording a concern afterwards. In the reference implementation that is three settings and one command:

fssaira oversight profiles/your_profile.yaml \
  --reviewers 11 --max-approvals 60 --deliberation-floor 45 --sweep

The tool refuses an approval returned faster than the floor, refuses one beyond the quota, escalates to a second reviewer under sustained load, and reports what it deferred. It also checks whether your quota and your reading time contradict each other, which is a mistake we shipped ourselves and found only by sweeping the parameters.

What this is not. No reviewer was observed to build any of this. The reading time is a number you declare, and the ceiling is only as honest as that declaration. Whether real reviewers degrade the way the model assumes is unmeasured and named as open work.
